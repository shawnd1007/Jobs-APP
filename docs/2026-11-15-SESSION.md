## What was observed

- A real résumé was imported on the device and the AI created a career strategy. The backend now tolerates minor quote formatting differences and omits individual suggestions whose evidence cannot be verified instead of discarding the whole strategy; the app reports the omission count.
- The Settings reset action clears recruiter state, including the résumé text, strategy, recruiter preferences, cached advice and matches. It preserves separate saved jobs, application tracking and connection settings. The backend reset was verified; the new Settings control still needs its own physical-device confirmation.
- The fresh test workspace initially had no watched boards. The 29 source identifiers from the earlier Mac workspace were restored into its settings; old job records and applications were not copied. This is local configuration, so a new or Azure workspace needs its own source setup. Boards can be added in workspace administration and their current health should be verified, not assumed.
- A completed source run reported no source failures and saved 35 openings for review. AI comparison completed for 12 openings: five needed confirmation and seven were not recommended. Another 58 openings were deferred by the current per-run comparison cap. These results do not establish that there are no suitable jobs, and no job application was submitted.
- The initial AI request failed after the local server restart because the Python process lacked its certificate-store setting. Configuring the system certificate store restored a verified secure connection. The résumé was not resent merely for that connection check.
- A local OpenAI key was exposed in chat during the session. Rotate or revoke that key and update the local configuration; do not copy the key into source, notes, logs or GitHub.
- Azure App Service setup remained blocked by inconsistent quota validation. A support request was reported submitted; verify its current status and billing directly before proceeding. Do not assume a deployed backend, database, working cloud sign-in or a hard spending cutoff.

## Current limitations and next steps

- The recruiter limits each comparison run to 12 openings and a shared daily AI-attempt ceiling; a retry repeats source discovery. Make complete-search coverage, continuation and failure recovery clear before treating a run as finished.
- A personal testing override, `JOBS_PERSONAL_UNLIMITED_AI=1`, removes the application-level daily AI attempt and recruiter comparison caps while retaining attempt counts. It must be explicitly configured on the backend; it is not enabled by default. Provider limits, other discovery limits and charges still apply. Do not use this setting for customer accounts.
- The recruiter shortlist shows categories and explanations; it does not show the older numerical fit score. Decide how to explain scores and supporting evidence without presenting them as hiring probabilities.
- Add meaningful in-app progress, location suggestions, and a user-managed original résumé in the candidate profile. Measure recruiter input and output token usage and estimated cost, including retries, before assessing subscription feasibility. These are roadmap requests, not completed features.
- Explore one complete search per week for customers while leaving applications and tracking available; maintain a separate testing allowance for Shawn. Define partial-failure recovery and validate costs before setting a policy or price.
- The current local prototype is a single workspace without production user isolation. Authentication, authorization, durable hosting, backup, privacy controls and multi-user security must be designed and verified before public release.
